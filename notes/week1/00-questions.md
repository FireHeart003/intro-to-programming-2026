# Types of Questions

1. Your stuff is broke, you need something now: **UNMUTE AND ASK ME**
2. A question that can be handled later goes in the parking lot.
    - these will be answered later, and you remove them when you feel you are answered.
    - if my answering your question gives you another question, either unmute or add it to the parking lot.
3. At the end of the day (4:30 ....) you can hang out and talk, ask any questions you want, discuss something we did in class, brag about your cats, whatever.

What doesn't work:

1. TEAMS CHAT. 
    - exception: Send me an email (jeff@hypertheory.com) if there is something you need that
       you don't want the whole class to be aware of.
2. Please do not do the raise your hand thing in teams. 



## Your Questions

You are almost never as lost as you think you are. One "dumb" question will change everything sometimes.
